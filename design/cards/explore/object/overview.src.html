LECTERN CARD DESIGN

Vitrine

Every card is a museum wall: the question is the label, the answer is the exhibit, and on the front its case stands empty in the shape of what you have to recall.

Room sign
Lecture number and lecture name, painted on the wall.
Marker
Exam weight. Filled when past exams asked it, outlined when core.
Label
The question in bold, then the medium line: role and answer shape.
Niche
The empty case on the front. Its shape is the answer's shape.
Exhibit
The answer, lit on a white panel in the same niche.
Extended label
Context, then the credit line with the source slide.
One typeface, one colour: the wall. Cards at 360 px, scaled to 55 %.
