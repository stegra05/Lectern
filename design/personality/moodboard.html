Personality in small details

Ten apps to react to. Each tile says what to look at and what Lectern could take from it. Answer with letters, for example "A and B, not G". The images load from the makers' own sites.

A

Things

Look at
Calm lists, generous space, one blue accent. Finishing a task feels good without any noise.
For Lectern
The quiet baseline: a native feel where craft shows in spacing and motion.
culturedcode.com/things
B

Flighty

Look at
A journey drawn as a path, with the details of a boarding pass and a departure board. The images here show little of that, so follow the link.
For Lectern
The course track as a journey: stops, progress along the line, arrival "In Anki".
flighty.app
C

Duolingo, the learning path

Look at
A winding path of coloured stops, one per step, with chunky pressable shapes.
For Lectern
The closest relative of the winding track. Also the loudest: a reference for how far play can go.
blog.duolingo.com
D

Linear

Look at
Precision and speed. Almost no decoration, personality through exactness. The image here is only a backdrop from their site, so follow the link.
For Lectern
The opposite pole to Duolingo: an instrument, not a companion.
linear.app
E

Notion

Look at
A plain, typographic tool whose personality sits in hand-drawn illustrations and friendly empty states. The image shows the plain side; the illustrations are on their site.
For Lectern
Personality kept to the edges: empty states, first run, the moment cards are sent.
notion.com
F

(Not Boring) apps

Look at
Bold colour and chunky 3D objects that make a plain utility feel like a game.
For Lectern
Play as the whole identity. Probably too much, but useful as a limit.
andyworks.co
G

Amie

Look at
Playful colour and small animations inside a calendar that still feels native. The image here is only their brand card, so follow the link.
For Lectern
Native controls with colourful content, which is the mix you described.
amie.so
H

Family

Look at
Tiny moments of delight: numbers that roll, buttons that respond, confirmations that feel earned. Brand card only, so follow the link.
For Lectern
Details on counts and confirmations, such as the 412 ticking up during the run.
family.co
I

Craft

Look at
Warmth and polish in a native document app: soft colour, careful type. Brand card only, so follow the link.
For Lectern
A warmer take on native than Things.
craft.do
J

Arc

Look at
A colour per space that follows you around, and a playful first run. Brand card only. Arc is being retired in December 2026, so it is a dated reference.
For Lectern
The idea behind lecture colours: a colour that means a place.
arc.net

Things, Duolingo and (Not Boring) show their interface well. Flighty, Linear and Notion show little of what makes them special, and G to J only have the maker's brand card, because those sites expose no interface image that can be shown here. For those the link is the real reference.
